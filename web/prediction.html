How the Prediction Works

DETERMINISTIC PIPELINE

Campus Environmental Intelligence utilizes an explainable linear regression calculation rather than opaque black-box heuristics, ensuring clear scientific auditability for student project evaluation.

01
Historical Readings

Recent MQ-135 sensor-response readings are collected directly from the sensor node rolling telemetry buffer.

02
Linear Regression (y = mx + b)

Ordinary least squares fits a straight line across recent points to determine the real-time slope and direction of reading drift.

03
Next Interval Extrapolation

The line projects 15 minutes ahead to produce the next discrete target response value.
